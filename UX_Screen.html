2.0 Update

One year after the app's launch, update 2.0 was released, focusing on enhancing the user experience (UX) and improving the visual and functional quality of the interface (UI). Thanks to user feedback and the experience gained in this field, we can highlight some of the improvements:

  • Complete redesign of the visual architecture, applying design principles, clear typographic hierarchy and a more accessible colour scheme consistent with the club's identity.
  • Optimisation of navigation flows, reducing unnecessary steps and applying more intuitive interaction patterns based on Nielsen heuristics.
  • Improvements in accessibility, including greater contrast, enlargement of tactile areas, and incorporation of micro-interactions that facilitate understanding of the interface.
  • Implementation of a more responsive and adaptive design, ensuring a smooth experience across different resolutions for both Android and iOS devices.
  • Complete review of the information architecture, reorganising key content (competitions, results, PDF documents, club links, etc.) to facilitate the location and prioritisation of information.
  • Optimisation of perceived performance, reducing loading times to minimise the feeling of waiting.
  • Refined iconography and interactive elements, using consistent, scalable components aligned with Human Interface Design guidelines.

VITTO APP

By Adrián Martín | September, 2024 - September, 2025

Is it possible to acquire good habits by using new technologies?

This final degree project addresses an issue that affects a high percentage of children in Catalonia, Spain and around the world, with negative consequences on both a physical and psychological level. This condition is linked to a sedentary lifestyle, poor diet and socio-economic factors. That is why innovative solutions are needed to promote healthy habits from an early age. This project proposes the design of the user experience (UX) and user interface (UI) of a gamified application aimed at children between the ages of 7 and 10.

The main objective is to create an interactive prototype that motivates children to incorporate physical activity and balanced eating routines through video game mechanics. Specific objectives include establishing a UX strategy tailored to children's motivation, developing game mechanics such as rewards, levels and avatar customisation, and designing clear, accessible and secure interfaces that are easy for the target audience to understand and use. In addition, the aim is to involve families and legal guardians through parental control and monitoring functions.

Working hours: +400h

My work: Research work, low, medium and high fidelity wireframe design. Prototyping, user personas, time management and testing phase implementation.
