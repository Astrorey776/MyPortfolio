About Me

Hi, I'm Adrian Martin, a passionate UI/UX Designer with a knack for creating user-centric designs that are not only visually appealing, but also highly functional. With a few years of experience working on various projects, I have been honing my skills in design, development and collaboration to deliver exceptional results.

I specialise in crafting engaging user interfaces, building interactive prototypes, and working closely with clients to achieve their goals with their projects. My portfolio showcases a variety of projects that highlight my dedication to design excellence, innovation and commitment to each project I work on.

When I'm not designing, I enjoy spending my free time swimming and teaching junior swimmers to perfect their swimming technique in the pool, my greatest hobby. I also spend some free time exploring new technologies, staying active and continuously learning to improve my craft.
